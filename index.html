hey, i'm encrize - an 18-year-old developer and cybersecurity specialist. i'm usually online, building something, taking something apart, or trying to figure out why it broke.

i make websites, apps, games, and low-level software, but most of my time goes into reverse engineering and malware analysis.

right now i'm working on fukOS, my own 32-bit x86 hobby OS written from scratch in c and assembly, along with fuk1 - a custom interpreted language designed to run on top of it.

i like projects that force me to understand what's actually happening under the hood. no magic, no giant frameworks - just code, mistakes, debugging.

feel free to explore my projects below or text me if you want to talk!
